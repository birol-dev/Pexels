Here are five facts about coffee that will change how you look at your morning cup.

Number one. Coffee beans are not beans. They are the seeds of a bright red fruit called a coffee cherry.

Number two. According to legend, an Ethiopian goat herder discovered coffee when his goats ate the cherries and would not stop dancing.

Number three. Finland drinks more coffee per person than any other country, about four cups a day.

Number four. A shot of espresso has less caffeine than a regular cup of filter coffee, because the serving is so small.

Number five. Coffee is one of the most traded farm products on Earth, and around twenty-five million small farms depend on it.

So the next time you take a sip, remember how far it travelled to reach you.
