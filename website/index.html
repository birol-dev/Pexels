What is StockFinder AI?

Free & open source
Version 1.4.0 · MIT
Windows · macOS · Linux

A free, open-source desktop app that splits your script into beats, finds matching Pexels photos and videos, and saves them to one folder with manifest.json and credits.

01How it works

Four steps. Zero stock-site spelunking.

  1. 01

    Paste your script

    Add your narration, a title, a platform and a visual mood.

  2. 02

    The agent maps visual beats

    Your LLM splits the script into beats with real queries, not one-word guesses.

  3. 03

    Pexels search and select

    It searches photos and videos and drops off-topic results. Approval mode lets you review first.

  4. 04

    Edit from a real folder

    Photos, videos, a manifest and credits in one folder. Drag it into Premiere, DaVinci or CapCut.

04Why “just search Pexels” fails

The problem isn't stock quality. It's translation.

Pexels has millions of clips. Your script speaks in story and metaphor. Hours vanish in the gap between the two.

Scripts speak in metaphor

“The market froze overnight” isn't a search for ice. Someone has to turn the line into shots Pexels can find.

Volume without structure

Downloading 30 clips is easy. Knowing which one goes with line 47 is the part StockFinder automates.

Context switching kills flow

Every minute on a stock site is a minute away from pacing, music and the cut.

12Questions

Before you download.

Is StockFinder AI a video editor?

No. It finds, downloads and organizes stock. You still edit in Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut or CapCut.

Do I need a Pexels API key?

Yes, and Pexels gives it out free. The key stays encrypted in your OS keychain and is never sent to us.

Which AI models are supported?

OpenAI, Google Gemini and OpenRouter (for Claude, Qwen and more). You bring your own key and model.

Is my script uploaded anywhere?

Only to the LLM provider you choose. There are no StockFinder servers. See the privacy statement.

Can I review assets before they download?

Yes. Turn on “require approval before download” in Settings and approve or reject each candidate. More in the workflow guide.

What about Pexels attribution?

Pexels asks you to credit photographers. StockFinder exports the credits for you. See the attribution guide.

Does it work on Mac and Linux?

Yes. GitHub releases has installers for Windows, macOS and Linux.

How much does it cost?

The app is free. You pay your LLM provider, usually cents per script, and Pexels is free. Details on the pricing page.

What does open source mean for me?

All the code is on GitHub. Check what it does, build it yourself or fork it.
