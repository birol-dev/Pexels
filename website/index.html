A few useful details

Before you download.

Is it really free?

The app is free and open source. You pay your AI provider for API usage; costs depend on your model and script. See costs.

What do I need to get started?

A free Pexels API key and an API key from OpenAI, Gemini, or OpenRouter. The setup guide walks you through it.

Does it work with my editor?

It downloads standard photos and video files. Import them into Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut, CapCut, or your preferred editor.

Where does my script go?

Your script is sent to the AI provider you choose. Downloads stay on your computer. Read the privacy details.
